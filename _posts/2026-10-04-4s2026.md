I'll be at the [2026 Society for Social Studies of Science conference](https://www.4sonline.org/meeting.php) in Toronto next week.
I'll be presenting some work I've been developing at OLS (Open Life Science Limited) on researcher positionality and the use of generative AI in research software engineering. It will be part of the panel organised by Ranjit Singh on The Craft of Science with AI: Evidence, Judgment, and Practice, at 8am on Friday. 
See you there?

If you can't make it--or you'd like a sneak peak--this presentation grew out of a position paper for a Research Software Alliance workshop in March on [‘Research Software Engineering in the Age of Generative AI: Building a Community Vision,’](https://www.researchsoft.org/events/rse-ai-workshop/) which you can read [here](https://zenodo.org/records/19054204).
